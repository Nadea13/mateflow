# Mateflow — Global All-in-One Backoffice & ERP

## Purpose & Target Audience
Mateflow is a high-density, mission-critical Backoffice & Operations Hub built for international cross-border merchants, wholesale businesses, and global DTC brand operators.

## Design Archetype & Tone
- **Mode:** Operate (High-density, low-friction, high scannability)
- **Tone:** Precise, calm, trustworthy, razor-sharp execution.
- **Anti-patterns strictly avoided:** AI slop, purple-blue gradient clichés, nested cards, fuzzy decorative glows, vague kickers.
- **Design Inspiration:** Linear, Stripe Dashboard, Raycast.

## Visual Tokens & Fundamentals
- **Primary Color:** Deep Slate Navy (`#090d16` dark / `#0f172a` light) paired with Electric Cyan/Sky precision accents (`#0284c7` / `#38bdf8`) and Emerald status green.
- **Typography:** Inter / IBM Plex Sans Thai with clean hierarchy and real numerical tabular figures (`font-feature-settings: "tnum"`).
- **Surface Elevation:** Refined 1px tinted borders (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)` light, `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.7)` dark) with soft layered shadows.
